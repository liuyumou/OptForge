# ADR-0023: Repeated-Solve Throughput Sessions and Budget Authority

Status: accepted by the maintainer's explicit implementation request, 2026-09-20.
Scope: GPUP-THROUGHPUT, a bounded research slice before GPUP-6.

## Decision

The GPUP-5C report showed the remaining LP solve cost is outside arithmetic: context
creation is 208-330 ms of a 400-600 ms certified solve at study size, and at 65536 rows
preconditioning (about 940 ms) and research preparation (about 1020 ms) each exceed the
19-30 ms of total kernel time in a 200-proposal run. Every solve today is one fresh
process that pays all of it. This slice adds an opt-in repeated-solve session that pays
those setup costs once per batch, without changing any existing runtime selector,
default, code object, schema meaning, seed, split, threshold or archived evidence.

Two paths implement [SPEC-0014](../specifications/SPEC-0014-lp-repeated-solve-throughput.md).
The public path is `cuda-lp-tuned` through a new opt-in session API
(`TunedSession`) and a new `optimizer solve-batch` CLI command. The research path is
the feature-gated isolated research executable through a `ResearchSession` and new
`batch`/`verify-batch` actions, limited to the two modes already proven bitwise
identical to the FP64 control: intguard-fp64 and fused-fp64. `Backend::solve`, backend
selectors, defaults and every existing schema's meaning are unchanged; no dispatch or
preset may route through a session.

A batch fixes one matrix, objective and row bounds. Solves are identical repeats, or
per-solve variable-bound variants generated by tightening bounds toward the instance's
frozen witness so the witness stays feasible; the generator is the SHA-256 counter PRNG
with a recorded seed band, min(n, 256) changed variables and dyadic fractions, with the
prefix property across batch sizes, and variant batches are refused for witness-less
instances. Every batch solve starts from a fresh controller and the cold iterate, is
certified host-side on the materialized model, and must be bitwise identical — primal
and dual bits, iteration and restart counts, status, launch count — to a fresh-process
single solve of the same materialized model, settings, code-object bytes and device;
identity is verified from evidence and reported as counts, never asserted.

Amortized throughput is a separately labeled endpoint. Fresh-process single-solve
latency remains the primary endpoint (SPEC-0013 requirements 10-13, ADR-0013); warm
timings are secondary diagnostics, and no report may substitute or tabulate amortized
per-solve time as single-solve latency.

## Frozen experiment and resources

Grid on the development partition plus the extension manifest, tier 1e-9, three
repetitions, configurations research-fused-fp64, research-intguard-fp64 and tuned-fp64
(CLI, development only): A fresh singles over the nine fast development instances; A'
fresh 200-proposal singles on scheduling-50x10, ta001 and ta011; B batch-repeat on the
nine fast instances at N in {1, 8, 32}; C batch-variant on the eight witness instances
at N in {1, 8, 32}; D batch-repeat-short on the three slow instances at N in {8, 32};
E fresh 200-proposal singles on the four extension instances with research-fused-fp64;
F batch-repeat-short and batch-variant-short on the four extension instances with
research-fused-fp64 at N = 8; G fresh single solves of variants 0 and 7 on the eight
witness instances. The frozen protocol file records the exact count (705 planned
attempts). Extension blocks are ordered last so cap exhaustion drops the least central
cells and records them as missing. Seeds 17/17001/17002, variant seed base 47100,
controller settings, randomized blocking, resource limits and the development partition
are unchanged. No validation/held-out data. Failed and incomplete runs remain.

All GPU work, including acceptance and failed attempts, uses the shared lease and the
original screening ledger under a new identity `gpup-throughput-v1` (prefix gpupt,
output artifacts/gpup-throughput-v1, acceptance artifacts/gpup-throughput-acceptance).
Preserve the 6722-event prefix SHA-256
ff6f1b5471e80791e964d1e6679017ea33593a7899718d0431f666cfaa5fa23b and 3794758955639
charged nanoseconds. The cumulative ceiling rises by 3600 seconds to 8102574545061
nanoseconds (4502574545061 + 3600e9); cap new study work at 2700 seconds and acceptance
at 600 seconds, with a combined 3300-second cap inside the new ceiling and unchanged
campaign (28800 s) and phase caps. Because the frozen v1/v2 studies pin the source
campaign's cumulative_gpu_ns, the ceiling authority moves into the new protocol:
benchmarks/configs/lp-throughput-v1.json (research-protocol schema version 3) carries
source_campaign {path, sha256}, additional_gpu_seconds and decision {path, sha256}
naming this ADR, and tooling refuses to open the ledger unless the new ceiling equals
the source cumulative plus the addition and the decision hash matches;
benchmarks/configs/lp-precision-v4.json, lp-research-v1.json and lp-research-v2.json
stay byte-identical. Admit complete deadline/cleanup reservations before work.
Exhaustion stops with explicit missing evidence, never another allowance. Four build
jobs; four-hour CPU validation ceiling.

## Documentation and acceptance

Require: full pinned-native tools/check.sh after the documentation phase and again on
final source; strict evidence checks for the new batch documents; bitwise device
differentials of batch solves against fresh single solves for both research modes and
cuda-lp-tuned; fault, admission, poisoning and cancellation session tests; all four
scoped sanitizers over every session_ case sequentially; independent verification of
every admitted attempt; and a report with the complete setup-cost breakdown, per-solve
costs, amortized throughput versus batch size, the paired ratio of amortized per-solve
time to fresh single-solve latency labeled as separate endpoints, bitwise identity
counts, coverage and missing attempts, and the full budget accounting. A negative or
null throughput result is valid evidence. No supported preset, dispatch rule, adaptive
recovery, quantization, warm start or runtime tolerance change follows from this slice.
Update SPEC-0012/SPEC-0013 cross-references, the roadmap, the candidate register,
operations, README, schemas README, wiki status, AGENTS and the CHANGELOG in the same
change as the behavior.
