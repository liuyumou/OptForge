# ADR-0074: Resolve availability and finish the complete confirmation

Status: accepted through the user's active goal, "Complete the confirmation".
Implementation: [SPEC-0041](../specifications/SPEC-0041-mixed2-confirm2.md).

SPEC-0040 produced useful exact numerical and supervision evidence but only
324/512 complete sessions. An incomplete result does not complete the requested
confirmation. Continue with bounded diagnosis of the actual missed intervals,
admit a supported availability correction, then execute a fresh frozen cohort.
Earlier evidence and all failed charges remain exact. The numerical executable,
image, original-model authority and complete-cost endpoints remain unchanged.

Successful completion means full coverage and a verified performance decision,
which may be unfavorable. It does not mean forcing a speedup or approving a
default. The user subsequently authorized changing or waiving an inappropriate
100 ms limit. Adopt a finite prospective one-second bound, retain every measured delay
and the old 100 ms exceedances, and record the resource tradeoff in
[the report](../benchmarks/lp-mixed2-confirm2.md) before freezing.
Do not regrade history or claim continuous memory enclosure from sampled RSS.
New independent funding and stronger operational admission precede execution.
