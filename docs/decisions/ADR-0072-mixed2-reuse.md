# ADR-0072: Diagnose reliability and confirm bounded changing-model reuse

Status: accepted through the user's instruction to proceed, 2026-11-13.
Implementation: [SPEC-0039](../specifications/SPEC-0039-mixed2-reuse.md).

The previous FP4 study demonstrates promising larger prepared arithmetic but
incomplete confirmation, short-session setup losses and unresolved reliability
events. Filling its missing block would not establish useful reuse. Preserve it
unchanged and use a new allocation for diagnosis and a prospective independent
cohort. Keep the watchdog; test bounded worker placement and record limitations.

Measure a four-scenario cache with changing original RHS/objective and independent
certificate authority. Charge its full preparation, switching and teardown to
both controls. A constructed 32-request stream is a bounded reuse experiment,
not proof of arbitrary production workload value. Confidence bounds and absolute
saved time determine the scoped finding; 1.10x remains provisional. No default
change, historical retry, automatic adoption or GPUP-9 release is authorized.
