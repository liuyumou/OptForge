# Billion-nonzero execution sequence

This is a development continuation under SPEC-0046 and its first amendment. The
approved target is one billion original matrix nonzeros in a connected LP. Keep
all six structural families and every capacity refusal. Construction witnesses
are separate objects and never enter the solve process.

The capacity ladder uses one FP4x2 solve per family at 64 million nonzeros, then
irregular and coupled families at 128, 256 and 512 million. These are full LP
inputs with 100-update development limits, original-model final assessment and
fresh-process timing. They establish executable capacity and cost, not convergence
or a comparison against native arithmetic. Preserve all earlier lower-scale and
public-model results; this ladder does not replace that roster.

After the ladder, generate the six one-billion-nonzero originals and freeze native,
FP32 and FP4x2 cells with identical inputs, strict 1e-9 certificates, 100-update
control and 500-update assessment. Initial maximum work is 5000 updates. Freeze
wall limits and complete reservations using the preceding capacity observations;
record all admitted, unsupported, interrupted, deadline and uncertified cells.
One rotated repetition is descriptive development evidence, not confirmation.

The new input reader hashes every original chunk and constructs CSR/CSC directly.
The scalar external checker uses the unchanged original dual enclosure and gap
criterion. It checks original feasibility in canonical row order. Every final and
intermediate endpoint remains available as immutable binary64 chunks. Final
checking is timed separately and included in complete single-solve cost; auditing
other assessments is separately recorded. Input generation is separately timed.

Only the pinned Pro6000 may execute GPU cells. No foreign process, zero observed
utilization and adequate free memory are required before reservation. Ongoing
ownership checks stop only this job's processes on foreign use or telemetry
failure. Whole-cohort budget and endpoint storage must fit before the first cell.
